Description

This two bedroom flat forms the upper part of a converted Victorian house on Burdett Road, Limehouse, and retains a number of period touches including high ceilings, original floorboards and sash windows.

The accommodation comprises a bright reception room, fitted kitchen, master bedroom, second bedroom, family bathroom and hallway with storage, extending to approximately 566 sq ft (53 sq m) in total.

The location is well placed for transport, with Mile End Underground and Westferry DLR both within easy walking distance, giving quick access into the City and Canary Wharf. Mile End Park and a range of local shops and amenities are also close by.

Offered part-furnished and available from 24 November 2026. Early viewings recommended.
